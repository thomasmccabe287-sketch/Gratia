What is Gratia MUN?

Gratia Model UN is an entirely student-led Model United Nations conference hosted by Incarnate Word High School. Gratia is focused on improving the youth of San Antonio through its environment of collaboration and diplomacy, as well as embodying Incarnate Word’s core values of compassion, integrity, service, stewardship, academic excellence, and community of faith.
