What is Gratia MUN?

Gratia Model UN is an entirely student-led Model United Nations conference hosted by Incarnate Word High School. Gratia is focused on improving the youth of San Antonio through its environment of collaboration and diplomacy, as well as embodying Incarnate Word’s core values of compassion, integrity, service, stewardship, academic excellence, and community of faith.

Theme of the Conference

As the state of international relations undergoes rapid change, affecting governments and citizens alike, the theme of GMUN ’26 becomes a reflection of the present day rather than simply an inspiration for conference material. Themed after the beloved Hunger Games books and movies, our conference will demonstrate the franchise’s aptitude for addressing difficult yet important topics: economic inequality, authoritarianism, censorship, and countless more. As participants, delegates are expected to navigate these same topics with grace. GMUN ’26 will shine a light on those around the world who have been neglected or marginalized and provide them with the opportunity for justice and equity through an environment of friendly debate. In GMUN’s second annual conference, we hope to foster an environment full of meaningful conversation that challenges perspectives and breaks barriers.
